## Candidate portraits

The five below appear as square thumbnails in the driver panels of
`/guides/f1-2027-haas-second-seat`, each rendering its photographer and licence
as links directly beneath it. That attribution is what the ShareAlike licences
require; CC0 needs none but is credited on the same terms.

Commons has no freely licensed portrait of Leonardo Fornaroli, and none of Ayao
Komatsu at all: the English Wikipedia article on Komatsu has no lead image and
his Wikidata item has no P18. Fornaroli is therefore shown in the Invicta car
he won the 2025 Formula 2 title in, and the Komatsu section carries no
photograph. Do not fill either gap with a press or agency photograph.
