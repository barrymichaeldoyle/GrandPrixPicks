# Instagram pinned posts

These three carousels are the permanent introduction to Grand Prix Picks. Together they answer the questions a new profile visitor will have:

1. What is this?
2. How does it work?
3. Why should I play with other people?

The intended profile-grid order is **What is Grand Prix Picks?**, **How scoring works**, then **Global and private competition**.

## Publishing sequence

Publish these manually across five days so each carousel has time to gather its own comments, saves and shares while the full profile introduction is still assembled quickly.

- **Day 1:** What is Grand Prix Picks?
- **Day 3:** How scoring works
- **Day 5:** Global leaderboard and private leagues

Pin each carousel as soon as it is live. Before publishing, select its first slide as the cover, paste the supplied caption, add the per-slide alt text and confirm the profile link is `https://grandprixpicks.com/ig`.

## How to publish pinned post 1

Use the Instagram mobile app so you can pin the finished post immediately.

1. Confirm that the profile website is `https://grandprixpicks.com/ig` and its title is **Play Grand Prix Picks**.
2. Tap **Create**, choose **Post**, then tap the multiple-image carousel control.
3. Select these four PNGs in filename order:
   - `01-cover.png`
   - `02-two-calls.png`
   - `03-two-tables.png`
   - `04-call-to-action.png`
4. Keep the first image as the cover and keep the portrait crop. Instagram applies one orientation to every image in a carousel.
5. Do not add a filter, crop, text overlay or border. The files are already exported at 1080 x 1350.
6. Keep the carousel silent. Music would not clarify this product introduction and may distract from the reading flow.
7. Paste the caption from the **Pinned post 1** section below. Do not paste a raw URL into the caption because it will not be clickable.
8. Open **Accessibility** or **Advanced settings**, depending on the app version, and add the matching alt text to each image.
9. Leave location blank. Do not tag unrelated F1 accounts or add a collaborator unless there is a real partnership.
10. Share the post, open it from the profile, tap the options menu and choose **Pin to your profile**.
11. Check the live post once: swipe through all four slides, confirm the text is not cropped, test the bio link and answer any genuine comments.

#### Slide 5

**Headline:** Correct team-mate call

**Body:** Pick the driver who finishes ahead in a team-mate matchup.

#### Slide 6

**Headline:** A perfect Top 5 earns 25 points

**Headline:** Picks lock when the session starts

**URL:** GrandPrixPicks.com/ig

### Caption

You do not need the exact finishing order to put points on the board.

Each Top 5 pick scores on its own:

- 5 points for the exact position
- 3 points for one place away
- 1 point if the driver still finishes in the actual Top 5
- 1 extra point for every correct team-mate call

A perfect Top 5 is worth 25 points before the team-mate picks are added. Picks lock at the scheduled start of each session.

Try the picker through the link in our bio.
