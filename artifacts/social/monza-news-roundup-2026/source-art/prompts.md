# Monza roundup source-art prompts

The source art was generated with the built-in image-generation tool on
28 August 2026. Exact factual copy, dates and branding were added separately by
the deterministic renderer; the generated layers contain no intended text,
logos, sponsors or real-driver likenesses.

Every image used the same core direction:

> A striking, sophisticated motorsport editorial collage for an independent
> magazine: cinematic Monza photography, tactile torn-paper edges, aged ivory,
> warm near-black, restrained screenprint grain, subtle halftone and real ink
> texture. Preserve calm dark negative space for separately rendered copy. No
> text, numbers, logos, sponsor marks, watermark, recognizable real driver,
> generic social template, neon, glossy 3D, gradients, giant circles or
> numbered-slide motifs.

## Cover

> The long Monza straight through dark parkland, old concrete banking, heat
> haze and a generic modern open-wheel car flashing through frame. Late Italian
> sunlight, red and green paint fragments, track-map marks and a tiny
> acid-chartreuse registration detail. Concentrate the action on the right and
> lower half.
